# LifeList privacy policy

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

LifeList is a to-do and life planner shown as a little island. It is built to keep your information on your iPhone.

## What LifeList stores
Everything you put into LifeList stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone. That includes tasks, crew, goals, budget, savings, journal, habits, your island layout and your health entries. LifeList has no account and no server, and it does not send your data anywhere.

## Apple Health
If you tap **Connect Apple Health**, LifeList asks iOS for permission to **read** these things:
- steps
- active energy (calories burned)
- workouts
- menstrual flow (cycle)

LifeList uses them only to fill in your Health Centre and your fitness streak on the phone. LifeList **never writes** to Apple Health and never asks for permission to write. Health data is not used for advertising or marketing. It is not shared with anyone and never leaves your device. You can turn access off at any time in **Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › LifeList**.

## Notifications
LifeList can remind you about bills, tasks that are due, repeating tasks and your focus timer. These reminders are scheduled on the phone itself. Turn them off in **Settings › Notifications › LifeList**.

## Backups
When you tap **Download backup**, LifeList makes one file and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose where that file goes (for example Files, AirDrop or Mail), and it goes only where you send it.

## Tracking, ads and analytics
LifeList has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Deleting your data
Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone. Backup files you saved yourself stay wherever you put them.

## Children
LifeList is not directed at children under 13.
